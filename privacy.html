Privacy Policy

Slow Sunday Games
Effective 24 November 2026

This privacy policy covers the four games made by Slow Sunday Games: Farmus Maximus, Fishus Maximus, Mosaicus Maximus and Legionus Maximus. It applies to every version of them: on Steam for Windows, Mac and Linux, on Google Play for Android phones and tablets, and on the App Store for iPhone and iPad.

Slow Sunday Games is a one-person game studio in Saskatoon, Saskatchewan, Canada. "We" and "us" in this policy mean Slow Sunday Games.

In short

  • The games collect no personal information. We have no player accounts and no servers, and we cannot see how you play.
  • Your progress and settings are saved on your own device.
  • There are no ads, no analytics, no tracking and no crash reporting in any of the games.
  • Purchases, achievements and cloud saves are handled by Steam, Google Play or the App Store, under their own privacy policies.
  • If you email us, we use your email only to answer you.

What the games keep on your device

Each game saves your progress and your settings as files on the computer, phone or tablet you play on. These include:

  • your saved games, with any names you type in, such as your farm's name or your dog's name;
  • your settings, such as volume, language, text size and controls;
  • on a phone or tablet, whether you have bought the full game, so that it stays unlocked when you are offline;
  • in Mosaicus Maximus, the floors you keep in your library.

These files stay on your device, apart from the copy Steam Cloud keeps if you use it (see below). We cannot see them, and we cannot recover them for you. Uninstalling a game, or clearing its data in your phone's settings, deletes them.

The first time a game starts, it looks at your device's language to choose its own, and that stays on your device too. On a computer, the games can play your own music from their music folder, and Mosaicus Maximus can save a picture of a floor to your Pictures folder when you ask it to. These files are only read and written on your computer.

What we collect

Nothing. The games contain no code that sends information to us or to anyone else, and we run no servers for them. Because there are no player accounts, we never learn who plays our games. The only time we receive personal information is when you email us (see Contacting us).

Steam

On Steam, the games use Steam's own software for:

  • achievements, which Steam keeps with your Steam account;
  • statistics: Legionus Maximus keeps the number of barbarians you have defeated as a Steam statistic, so that its achievements for defeating many of them count across all your saves and computers;
  • rich presence, which lets your Steam friends see what you are doing in the game, such as working at the market or fighting in Gaul. Your Steam privacy settings decide who can see it;
  • Steam Cloud, where a game uses it and you have it turned on, which keeps a copy of your saved games so you can carry on on another computer.

Steam also records what it records for every game, such as how long you play. All of this is handled by Valve under the Steam Privacy Policy Agreement. The games play normally without Steam running; your achievements then stay in your save.

Some buttons open a web page, such as a game's page on the Steam store. That page belongs to Valve.

Google Play

On Android, each game is a free download, and one in-app purchase unlocks the full game. Google Play handles that purchase from start to finish, and we never see your name, card or payment details. The game only asks Google Play for the price and whether you own the full game, and remembers the answer on your device. Google Play's billing software may also send Google technical details about the purchase, under Google's own policy. If the purchase is refunded, Google Play tells the game and the full game locks again.

Google Play Games (Farmus Maximus only)

Farmus Maximus can send its achievements to Google Play Games. When the game first starts, Play Games may offer to sign you in. This is optional: if you say no, the game will not ask again, and it plays exactly the same, with your achievements kept in the game. If you sign in, Google keeps the achievements you earn with your Play Games profile, along with your gamer name and picture. The Play Games software in the game may also send Google technical information it needs to keep Play Games working. You can choose who sees your Play Games profile, or delete it with its game data, in the Play Games app or your Google Account. The other three games do not use Play Games.

Reminders (Farmus Maximus on phones and tablets)

Farmus Maximus can remind you when the cart is home, when the storehouse is full or when the builders have finished. These reminders are set and shown by your phone itself, and nothing about them is sent over the internet. The game only asks for permission to show them when you tap Allow under Settings > Notifications, and each kind of reminder can be turned off there, or all of them in your phone's settings. The other three games do not show notifications.

Google's handling of all of this is covered by the Google Privacy Policy.

App Store

On iPhone and iPad, the purchase that unlocks the full game is handled by Apple through the App Store, and we never see your payment details. Apple's handling of it is covered by the Apple Privacy Policy.

Reports the stores give us

Like every developer, we receive sales reports from Steam, Google Play and the App Store, so that we can keep our accounts and help with refunds. They never include your payment details. If you have chosen in your device's settings to share usage and diagnostic information with Google or Apple, they may also show us crash and performance figures for our games, without telling us who you are.

Android permissions

The Android version of each game lists these permissions:

  • Internet and network state: used by Google Play's billing software, and by Play Games in Farmus Maximus, to reach Google. The games themselves send nothing.
  • Google Play billing: for the purchase of the full game.
  • Notifications, and running after a restart: for Farmus Maximus's reminders, which are set again if your phone restarts. The other three games list these too, because all four share the same phone toolkit, but they never use them.

The games do not ask for your location, contacts, camera, microphone, photos or files, and do not use your device's advertising ID.

No ads, analytics or tracking

None of the games shows ads, and none ever will. They contain no advertising or analytics software, do not use your advertising ID, do not track you across other apps or websites, and do not build a profile of you. We do not sell, rent or share information about you with anyone, so no third party handles it on our behalf.

This website sets no cookies and runs no analytics. The company that hosts it may keep ordinary server logs, as every web host does.

Children

Our games are made for a general audience and are not directed at children under 13. Because the games collect no personal information from anyone, they collect none from children. Legionus Maximus has battles and shows blood when enemies fall; the blood can be turned off in its settings, and its store rating says which ages it suits.

If you are a parent or guardian and believe your child has sent us personal information by email, write to us and we will delete it.

Keeping information safe, and deleting it

Information on your device stays under your control, and deleting a game deletes its saves. Steam, Google and Apple protect what they handle with their own security and send it over encrypted connections.

Emails you send us are kept in a password-protected email account, only for as long as we need them to help you, and we delete them sooner if you ask. We hold no other information about you, so there is nothing else for us to delete. To remove what the stores keep, use their own settings: Steam Cloud in Steam, your Play Games profile or Google Account, or your Apple Account.

You can change your mind at any time: turn reminders off in the game or your phone's settings, sign out of Play Games, or turn off Steam Cloud and rich presence in Steam.

Your rights

Wherever you live, you can ask us what personal information we hold about you, and ask us to correct or delete it. In practice, the only information we could hold is an email you sent us. Write to us at the address below and we will answer within 30 days. If you are not happy with our answer, you can complain to the privacy authority where you live; in Canada, that is the Office of the Privacy Commissioner of Canada.

Changes to this policy

If a game changes what it does with information, we will update this page before the change reaches you, change the effective date at the top, and say so in the game's update notes on Steam, Google Play and the App Store. The date at the top always shows when this policy last changed.

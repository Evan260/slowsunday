Getting the full game back on a phone or tablet

On Android phones and tablets, iPhone and iPad, each game is a free download, and one purchase unlocks the full game. You buy it with the Unlock the full game button on the title screen.

If you have already bought it and the game shows as locked, for example after installing it again or on a new phone or tablet:

  1. Check that your device is signed in with the account you bought it with: your Google account on Android, or your Apple Account on iPhone and iPad.
  2. Connect to the internet and open the game. It often finds your purchase on its own after a moment.
  3. If the full game is still locked, open Settings and tap Restore purchase, next to "Already bought the full game?".

The button is only there while the full game is locked, so once it is unlocked you won't see it any more.

When the store finds your purchase, the game says "The full game is unlocked again. Welcome back!" If it says "No purchase of the full game was found for this account", check which account your device is signed in with. If it says "The store cannot be reached right now", check your connection and try again a little later.

A purchase belongs to the store you made it in. Bought on Google Play, it unlocks the game on Android devices signed in with that Google account. Bought on the App Store, it unlocks the game on iPhones and iPads signed in with that Apple Account. It doesn't carry over from one store to the other, or to Steam, where the full game is sold on its own.

If your purchase is waiting for approval, such as Ask to Buy or a family member's approval, or for a payment to go through, the game stays locked until the store confirms it, then unlocks.

Refunds

We don't take payments ourselves. Every purchase goes through Google Play, the App Store or Steam, and each of them decides refunds under its own rules.

  • Google Play: see Request a refund on Google Play. Sometimes Google Play asks you to contact the developer instead, for example once more than 48 hours have passed since you bought it. If so, email us with the order number from your Google Play receipt (it starts with GPA.) and we will look into it.
  • App Store: Apple handles every App Store refund, and we can't give them ourselves. See Request a refund for apps or content that you bought from Apple, or go straight to reportaproblem.apple.com.
  • Steam: see Steam Refunds.

If the purchase on a phone or tablet is refunded, the game goes back to the free version. Your saves stay as they are.

Your saves on a phone or tablet

Each game keeps your progress on the phone or tablet you play on, in the game's own storage. There are no accounts and no online saves, so we can't see your saves or bring them back for you.

  • Updates keep your saves.
  • Deleting the game, or clearing its storage in your device's settings, deletes its saves. On iPhone and iPad, Offload App keeps them and Delete App removes them.
  • A new Android phone or tablet: the games' saves are not part of Google's backup of your apps, so they usually stay on the old device and you start a new game on the new one. Your purchase comes with you: see Getting the full game back.
  • A new iPhone or iPad: your saves are part of the game's data on the device, which Apple includes in iCloud Backup and in backups made on a computer. If you set up the new device from a backup of the old one, or move everything across from the old device directly, your saves normally come along. A device set up as new starts fresh.
  • Saves don't move between Android and Apple devices, or between a phone and a computer.

Reporting a problem

If something in a game doesn't work as it should, email us. These details help us find it quickly:

  • The game: Farmus Maximus, Fishus Maximus, Mosaicus Maximus or Legionus Maximus.
  • Where you play it: on Steam (Windows, Mac, Linux or Steam Deck), from Google Play, or from the App Store.
  • Your device: the phone, tablet or computer, and which version of Android, iOS, iPadOS, Windows, macOS or Linux it runs.
  • The game's version: Farmus, Fishus and Mosaicus Maximus show it in the bottom right corner of the title screen, such as v1.0.0. Legionus Maximus doesn't show it in the game; on a phone or tablet, you'll find it in your device's settings, in the game's app details. If you can't find it, tell us roughly when you installed or last updated the game.
  • What happened: what you were doing, what you expected, and what happened instead. If it happens every time, the steps that lead to it.
  • A screenshot or a short screen recording, if you can.

For a question about a purchase, add the order number from your store receipt. We never need your password or payment details, so please don't send them.
